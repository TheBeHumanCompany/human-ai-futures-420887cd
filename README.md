# Human AI Futures

Build a responsive, premium website for The Be Human Company using the two uploaded screenshots as visual references.

Do not copy either screenshot exactly. Use the second screenshot as the primary visual direction because it feels cleaner, more editorial, modern and culturally relevant. Use the first screenshot as a reference for the range of content and the way Be Human AI services are explained.

ABOUT THE COMPANY

The Be Human Company is both:

1. An AI strategy and transformation company helping leaders and organizations prepare for the AI era.

2. A cultural movement encouraging people to practise the qualities that make us human.

Our central belief:

“Being human is what we’re born with.

Humanity is what we practise.”

Our mission is to help people and organizations become more capable with AI while becoming more intentional about their humanity.

PRIMARY BUSINESS OFFER

Be Human AI is currently our main commercial offering and must be easy to understand within the first screen and the first scroll.

Be Human AI helps organizations with:

- AI readiness and strategy

- Human-centred AI adoption

- Security, privacy and governance

- AI agents and workflow transformation

- Leadership, team culture and AI literacy

The website should clearly communicate that we provide real AI consulting and implementation services. It must not look like only a social movement, media company or philosophical project.

HOMEPAGE STRUCTURE

1. NAVIGATION

Logo: Be Human Company

Navigation:

- About

- Be Human AI

- The New Human Era

- The Human Archive

- Podcast

- Contact

Primary navigation button:

“Work With Us”

Keep the navigation minimal and sophisticated.

2. HERO

Headline:

“THE FUTURE IS HUMAN.”

Supporting copy:

“We help leaders and organizations become AI-ready without losing what makes them human.”

Primary CTA:

“Explore Be Human AI”

Secondary CTA:

“Watch Our Manifesto”

Use cinematic documentary-style imagery showing the relationship and tension between humanity and technology. AI and robots may appear, but they must not dominate the brand or be portrayed as a replacement for human beings.

The hero should feel bold, cinematic, intelligent and emotionally resonant.

3. BE HUMAN AI

Place this section directly after the hero so visitors quickly understand what the company sells.

Headline:

“Build an organization ready for the AI era.”

Supporting copy:

“We help organizations strengthen their people, protect what matters and transform how work gets done.”

Create four service cards:

- AI Readiness & Strategy

- Human + AI Transformation

- Security, Privacy & Governance

- AI Agents & Workflow Design

Include a CTA:

“Explore Be Human AI”

4. WHY WE EXIST

Use an editorial cream-background section.

Large copy:

“Being human is what we’re born with.

Humanity is what we practise.”

Supporting copy:

“As technology becomes more powerful, humanity must become more intentional.”

Include a documentary video placeholder and a link to read the manifesto.

5. THE NEW HUMAN ERA

Create a clean editorial section introducing the cultural ideas of the brand.

Use six principles:

- Presence is the new luxury.

- Wisdom is the new intelligence.

- Humanity is the advantage.

- Real is rare.

- Trust wins.

- Character is earned.

Do not make this section feel like generic motivational quote cards. It should feel like a modern cultural publication or an independent magazine.

6. THE HUMAN ARCHIVE

Headline:

“Real stories. Real humans.”

Description:

“A growing archive of conversations, experiences and perspectives exploring what it means to be human.”

Include a horizontal collection of documentary portraits with names, locations and story titles.

CTA:

“Explore the Human Archive”

7. PODCAST

Introduce the podcast as thoughtful conversations about leadership, AI, culture and building organizations where humanity becomes a competitive advantage.

Include:

- Featured episode

- Podcast image or microphone visual

- Listen button

- Space for recent episodes

8. FINAL CTA

Headline:

“Prepare your organization for the New Human Era.”

Buttons:

“Work With Be Human AI”

“Join the Movement”

9. FOOTER

Include all navigation, social channels, contact details and the sign-off:

“Stay Human.”

VISUAL DIRECTION

The brand should feel like:

- Apple meets Patagonia meets A24

- A cultural movement

- An independent magazine

- A documentary studio

- A timeless fashion brand

- A field journal from the future

Use:

- Black and warm cream backgrounds

- Off-white typography

- A restrained acid-lime accent colour

- Large condensed editorial headlines

- Clean sans-serif body typography

- Documentary photography

- Film grain and subtle texture

- Strong spacing and intentional negative space

- Thin borders and understated interactions

- Occasional handwritten or archival details

AVOID

- Generic SaaS gradients

- Excessive glowing AI effects

- Too many cards

- Cluttered dashboard elements

- Stock-photo corporate teams

- Futuristic blue and purple branding

- Overuse of robots

- Making humans and robots appear like equal companions

- Generic motivational branding

- Tiny text

- Overcrowded sections

- Making every section black

The final result should feel culturally relevant and emotionally human while still clearly selling serious AI services.

Before building the full website, show me three possible homepage design directions and explain how each one balances the cultural movement with the AI consulting business.
